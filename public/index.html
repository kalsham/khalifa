THE EDL

How it edits

Same pipeline a human editor runs — Scenepilot just runs it while you're already on to the next shoot.

  1. LOG

    Drop in your raw clips. Scenepilot logs every take — labels the keepers, flags the flubs, finds the room tone.

  2. CUT

    It assembles a rough cut paced for where it's going: punchy for a Reel, patient for a sit‑down interview.

  3. GRADE

    Exposure, white balance, and audio levels even out across every clip, so nothing jumps jarringly bright or loud.

  4. MIX

    Music ducks under dialogue, captions land in sync, and the export drops straight into your upload queue.
