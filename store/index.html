Brew guide — pour-over, timed

Three minutes, four moves.

Works for any bag on this page: 15g coffee, medium-fine grind, 250g water off the boil.

0:00 — 0:30

Bloom

Pour 40g water. Let the grounds swell and release CO₂.

0:30 — 1:15

First Pour

Slow spirals from the center out, up to 200g total.

1:15 — 2:00

Second Pour

Top up to 250g. This is the last water you add.

2:00 — 3:00

Drawdown

Let it fully drain. Swirl once, then serve.
