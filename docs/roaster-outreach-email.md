# Roaster Outreach — Templates

Working templates for pitching independent UAE roasters on a consignment
partnership with Souk Al-Bunn. Swap all bracketed fields before sending.
Personalize the first line of the email for every roaster — a pitch that
mentions their actual beans lands far better than one that doesn't.

---

## 1. Email — full pitch

**Subject:** Partner with Souk Al-Bunn — new sales channel, zero upfront cost

Hi [Roaster contact name],

I'm building **Souk Al-Bunn**, a new online marketplace launching in the UAE
that brings beans from a handful of independent roasters together in one
bilingual (English/Arabic) store — one checkout and one delivery, no matter
how many roasters a customer buys from.

I'm reaching out because [specific, genuine detail — e.g. "your Ethiopia
Guji is one of the best light roasts I've had in Dubai this year" /
"I keep seeing [Roaster] recommended in [specific community/forum]"], and
I'd like to include one of your bags in the launch lineup.

**How it works:**
- **Consignment, not wholesale** — you keep ownership of the stock until it
  sells; we pay out net-30 on what moves. No upfront cost to you.
- **We handle fulfillment** — storage, packing, delivery, and customer
  service for the online order. Your team doesn't touch it.
- **Full attribution** — every listing carries your name and roast profile.
  This is an incremental channel alongside your own site, not a replacement
  for it.
- **You set the price floor** — we agree the retail price together before
  anything goes live.

**What I'd need to start:**
- One flagship 250g bag — whichever roast you feel is most representative
- Visibility on roast date, so we can enforce a 60-day shelf-life cap on our
  end (freshness is the main thing we're building trust on)
- 15 minutes on a call to walk through details and answer questions

If this sounds interesting, I'd love to grab a short call this week or
next — happy to work around your schedule. Or just reply here and I'll send
over more detail.

Thanks for the coffee you're putting into the world — hope to help more
people find it.

[Your name]
[Phone / WhatsApp]
[Email]
Souk Al-Bunn

## Notes on use

- Send the email version to roasters found via their website/contact form;
  use the WhatsApp version for roasters you can reach informally (Instagram
  DM, a mutual contact, or after meeting at a market/pop-up).
- Track responses somewhere simple (a spreadsheet: roaster, contact,
  channel, date sent, status) — with 8–12 outreach attempts targeting 5–8
  signed partners, you'll want to know who still needs a follow-up.
- A follow-up after 4–5 days with no reply is normal and expected — keep it
  to one line: "Following up on this in case it got buried — still happy to
  do a quick call whenever works."
