# Launch Checklist

## Before listing anywhere

- [ ] Open `UGC-Creator-Business-OS.xlsx` yourself, fill in 3–4 fake rows
      per tab, confirm the Dashboard numbers update and nothing shows an
      error (#REF!, #VALUE!, #DIV/0!)
- [ ] Open it in both Google Sheets AND Excel/LibreOffice once — dropdowns
      and conditional formatting occasionally behave differently across
      apps, catch it before a customer does
- [ ] Take 4–6 clean screenshots for the listing: Dashboard, Pipeline (with
      color-coded stages visible), Rate Calculator, Usage Rights Cheat Sheet
- [ ] Read through `UGC-Creator-Quick-Start-Guide.pdf` once end to end

## Lemon Squeezy (primary storefront — no trade license required)

- [ ] Create a Lemon Squeezy account and store (individual creator, not a
      registered business — this is the point of using a Merchant of Record)
- [ ] Create the product using `listing-copy.md` title/description (use the
      Gumroad short-title version)
- [ ] Price: list the Pro Bundle only at $39 to start — simpler to manage
      as one SKU than running Base + Pro in parallel
- [ ] Upload the spreadsheet + PDF guide as the digital files attached to
      the product
- [ ] Add the screenshots as product images (first image is what shows in
      any shared link preview — make it the Dashboard, it's the most visual)
- [ ] Turn on email capture at checkout so every buyer joins your list —
      this matters for selling your NEXT product to the same audience later
- [ ] Grab the checkout link — this is what goes in your bio and every
      video's link

## Content (the actual growth engine — do this, don't skip it)

Without Etsy's search traffic, this is not optional supporting work — it's
the entire top of the funnel.

- [ ] Record and post the 5 video hooks from `listing-copy.md`, one per day
      for the first 5 days
- [ ] Put the Lemon Squeezy link in bio; mention it once at the end of each
      video, don't make the whole video an ad
- [ ] After day 5, look at which hook got the most engagement — make 2 more
      videos in that specific angle

## Later, optional

- [ ] If a trade license comes through, add Etsy as a second channel for
      search-driven discovery — additive, not required to keep selling

## Two weeks after launch — decide, don't guess

- [ ] Check actual sales count and which price point/bundle sold
- [ ] Read any reviews/DMs for what's missing — this is your real tier-2
      roadmap, not a guess made now
- [ ] Decide: double down on this niche with a second product (e.g. a
      contract template pack), or take the same method to a new sub-niche
