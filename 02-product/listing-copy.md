# Etsy / Gumroad Listing Copy

## Mentor note on why this copy is structured this way

Digital product listings sell on **specificity of the pain, not
cleverness of the copy**. The title carries SEO keywords (what people
actually type into Etsy search). The description leads with the exact
mistake the buyer is currently making, because "here's a tracker" is
generic — "here's what's costing you $200+ per deal" is a reason to buy
right now.

## Description

**The #1 mistake new UGC creators make isn't pricing too low — it's not
knowing WHY a rate should change from deal to deal.**

If you've ever accepted a brand's first offer, then found out later they
ran your video as a paid ad for 6 months... you got paid once for
something they used for months. That's not a haggling problem. That's a
tracking problem.

**UGC Creator Business OS** is the spreadsheet that turns your brand deal
side hustle into a business you can actually see the numbers on:

✔️ **Brand Deal Pipeline** — every conversation, from first DM to paid
invoice, in one CRM-style tracker (color-coded by stage)

✔️ **Rate Calculator** — stop guessing. Enter the deliverable type and
usage rights the brand wants, get a suggested rate instantly, built on
transparent math you can defend in a negotiation

✔️ **Invoice Log** — track what's paid, what's overdue (auto-flagged),
and your total revenue by month/quarter/year — automatically, from the
same data you're already entering

✔️ **Content Calendar** — never miss a filming or posting deadline again

✔️ **Usage Rights Cheat Sheet** — the plain-English guide to what
"whitelisting," "usage rights," and "buyout" actually mean, and what each
one is worth — this alone is worth more than the price of the template

**Works in Google Sheets AND Excel.** No app to learn, no subscription,
instant download.
