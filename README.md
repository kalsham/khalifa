# Khalifa — Digital Products Mentorship

This repo is the business plan and curriculum for a **digital products education
business**: you teach people how to create and sell their own digital
products (courses, templates, communities, software), and you monetize that
teaching itself as a digital product. This is the same playbook Iman Gadzhi
runs today (having moved on from pure SMMA into "GrowYourAgency" /
education-as-a-product) — the person selling the picks-and-shovels, not just
swinging one.

## Why this model

Three reasons this beats "build one digital product and hope it sells":

1. **You're the proof.** Every product you build to teach the method also
   becomes a case study for the method. The teaching business and the
   product business compound each other.
2. **Infinite niches, one skillset.** The curriculum you're about to read
   applies whether the "digital product" being taught is a Notion template
   business, a fitness app, or a paid Discord community. You're teaching the
   operating system, not one app.
3. **Margins.** Digital products have ~90%+ gross margin. No inventory, no
   fulfillment cost per unit, no agency-style labor scaling problem.

## How this repo is organized

- **`01-business-plan/`** — the actual business: who you sell to, what you
  sell them, how much it costs, how they find you, how the money works.
  Read this first — it's the "why" behind every curriculum decision.
- **`02-curriculum/`** — the product itself: the course/mentorship content
  you sell. Each module is written as a deliverable-ready outline you can
  turn directly into videos, a PDF, or a cohort call.
